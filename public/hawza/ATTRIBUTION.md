## Why this image

A real madrasa (مدرسهٔ علمیه) courtyard with its sunken courtyard, iwân and
student rooms — the architectural archetype of Iranian seminary education.
It is deliberately **not** tied to any single person or institution, so the
login identity reads as «سامانهٔ آموزش و دانش حوزوی» (a seminary education
platform) rather than a memorial or a specific school. CC0 keeps licensing
trivial.
